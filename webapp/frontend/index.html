      Draw a rectangle on the map, then generate a grid of tree points inside it.

      L→R + B→T ⇒ R1·T1 in the SW corner. Flip either independently to move it.

      Leave empty to fit the rectangle. Bump after Generate to grow the grid.

      Hold Shift and drag on the map to rotate interactively.

      Double-click any tree to use it as the pivot.

      Prefix: —

      Masks stack — apply several to iteratively clean up. Positive keeps points inside; negative removes points inside. Row/T numbers are renumbered after each run.
